Dr. Salim Ali

The Birdman of India

(1896–1987)

“

"My chief interest in bird study has always been its ecology, its life history under natural conditions."

Pioneer of Indian Ornithology

Dr. Salim Moizuddin Abdul Ali was the first Indian to conduct systematic bird surveys across the entire subcontinent. His monumental books and conservation efforts transformed how India views its natural wildlife and protected habitats.

The Spark

A childhood encounter with a yellow-throated sparrow sparked an unusual curiosity, routing his path from a hesitant student into the premier naturalist of Asia.

The Field Nomad

Traveling across remote forests, princely states, and high-altitude Himalayan ranges, he meticulously recorded species data that formed modern conservation baselines.

Saving Sanctuaries

His influential leadership helped safeguard priceless ecosystems like Bharatpur (Keoladeo National Park) and the Silent Valley from industrial destruction.
